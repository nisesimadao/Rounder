What it does

Rounder draws borderless corner overlays above the desktop, then gets out of the way. Settings stay local, and everything lives in the menu bar.

01

No permissions

No Accessibility permission, Screen Recording, Automation, or network access.

02

Live tuning

Radius, color, corner selection, presets, and glow changes apply while running.

03

Multi-display

Choose which displays get rounded and keep external monitor desks consistent.

R 40

Under the hood.

Engineering

The overlays sit above the menu bar and Notification Center, so the very edge of the screen rounds cleanly.

01

Clicks pass through

The overlays ignore mouse events. Menus and buttons under the corners keep working.

02

Survives fullscreen

Corners stay attached across Spaces, desktop switches, and full-screen apps.

03

Draws only the corners

Four tiny windows instead of a full-screen sheet, with paths cached for low CPU use.

04

Follows your displays

Recalculates positions when the resolution changes or displays are plugged in.
