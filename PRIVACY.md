# Privacy Policy

Rounder is a local macOS utility.

## Data Collection

Rounder does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, personal data, or usage data.

## Network Access

Rounder does not require network access for its core functionality.

## Local Storage

Rounder stores settings locally using macOS `UserDefaults`, including:

- Corner radius, color, and shape
- Enabled corners
- Selected displays
- Presets
- Gaming-mode settings
- Launch-at-login preference

These settings remain on your Mac unless you export or share them yourself.

## Permissions

Rounder is designed to work without Accessibility, Screen Recording, Automation, Location, Camera, or Microphone permissions.
